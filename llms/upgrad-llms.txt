# upGrad Education

## Summary
upGrad is a leading online education platform offering industry-relevant courses, degrees, and certifications. It provides programs in Data Science, MBA, AI & ML, Digital Marketing, Management, Doctorate, Generative AI & Agentic AI, and Study Abroad.
For study abroad aspirants, upGrad offers university selection, application assistance, visa guidance, and exam preparation for IELTS, GRE, GMAT, TOEFL, PTE, and Duolingo. Students can explore courses and top universities in destinations like the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Ireland, and more.

## Detailed Information
- **Topics Covered:** Data Science, MBA, AI & ML, Generative AI & Agentic AI , Digital Marketing, Management, Doctorate and Study Abroad.
- **Target Audience:** Students, working professionals, fresh graduates, study abroad aspirants, career switchers, individuals preparing for their first job, and those seeking an international degree.
- **Course Format:** Online self-paced or instructor-led courses - both free and paid, with certifications, live mentorship, and career support.
- **Key Partners:** Leading global universities and industry experts.
